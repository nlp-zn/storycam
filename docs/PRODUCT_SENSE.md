# Product Sense

## Positioning

StoryCam is an AI private story theater for ordinary users. It is not an industrial short-drama production backend.

## Product Taste

- Prefer emotional clarity over production complexity.
- Let users recognize their story before video generation.
- Keep professional directing methods internal.
- Build trust before external provider calls.
- Favor a narrow magical path over a broad unfinished workstation.

## Phase 1 Non-Goals

- Public sharing links.
- Payments.
- Marketplace.
- Public feed.
- Mobile-only experience.
- Professional shot table export.
